🕹️ Controls Reference:
• Touchscreen: Tap any bottom action button (FOLD, CHECK/CALL, RAISE, +BB, ALL-IN), tap table to deal next hand, or tap top-right to toggle audio.
• F: Fold  |  C: Check / Call  |  R: Bet / Raise  |  A: All-In
• W / Z or + / - / D-Pad Up/Down: Adjust raise target by 1 Big Blind.
• D or H: Deal Hand  |  B: Cycle Blinds ($5/$10, $10/$20, $25/$50)  |  N: New Table ($500 buy-in)
• Enter / Space / Trackball Click: Activate highlighted button (or skip AI delay).
• M: Mute/Unmute sound synthesizer.
